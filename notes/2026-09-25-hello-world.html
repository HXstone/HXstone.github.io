Hello, World: My Personal Digital Space

2026-09-25 中文 / EN

Recording growth, exploring technology — and leaving a voice that is my own.

About me

Hello — I'm a high school student. As I've grown up, I've found myself increasingly drawn to artificial intelligence, finance, football and distance running. These interests have not only enriched my life; they keep pushing me to explore a wider world.

Why I built this site

In an age when information moves so quickly, we're used to expressing ourselves through mainstream platforms like WeChat, X and Douyin — but those platforms are shaped by algorithms, formats and rules of distribution. So I wanted to build a digital space of my own. This is not simply a page that displays information: it's a place where I can freely record my thinking, share my views and keep a trace of my growth. With AI and internet technology, I hope my voice can be recorded and carried beyond social media.

What I'll share here

In the future I'll share thoughts on AI, technology, international news and personal growth. There may be observations on new technology, summaries from my studies, reflections on daily life, and the way I understand a changing world. I hope these words become a record of my ongoing exploration.

A letter to my future self

In the next few years I don't know where I'll end up, or what challenges I'll meet. But I hope I'll always stay curious, keep learning, keep trying new things, and grow through the process of exploring. This website is a record I'm leaving for my future self. When I open it again years from now, I hope I'll see someone who keeps improving and keeps changing.
