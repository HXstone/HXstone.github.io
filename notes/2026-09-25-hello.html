The Site Goes Live

2026-09-25 中文 / EN Learning

The site is up. Consider this the opening entry — why it exists, and how I made it.

Why I built it

I make small things: Minesweeper, Dino Run, and a mistake notebook I'm still organising. They were scattered across folders on my computer, and showing them to anyone meant digging around. I wanted one fixed place to put my work and my notes.

What will be here

  • Projects — small games and whatever comes next, most of them open straight in the browser.
  • Notes — study logs, tinkering records and half-formed ideas.
  • About — what I'm working on, and how to reach me.

How I made it

Honestly, I don't write code the way an engineer does. My method is: describe what I want as clearly as I can, let AI implement it, test it in the browser again and again, and keep asking for changes wherever it feels wrong.

The site was tuned into shape piece by piece this way. So if something looks good, AI deserves most of the credit; if something looks off, it's probably because I didn't describe it well enough :)

Making the thing, and putting it somewhere it can be seen — that is a kind of completion in itself.

What's next

I'll keep working on the mistake notebook, build new small things, and record the process here. If you'd like to talk, email me.

Let's build something.
